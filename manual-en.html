1.3 Adding Customers & Applying Discounts
👤 Adding a Customer
1
Tap "Add Customer"
Located at the top of the Order Summary panel.
2
Search or Create
Type the customer's phone number or name to search. If they're new, tap Create New Customer.
3
Fill In Details
First name is required. Last name and phone number are optional but help for future lookups.
⚠️
Reminder If the customer already exists in the system, always search first to avoid duplicates.
💰 Applying a Discount
1
Tap "Discount"
Found in the bottom action bar of the Order Summary.
2
Choose Discount Type
Select either Peso Amount (e.g., ₱50 off) or Percentage (e.g., 10% off).
3
Enter the Value & Confirm
The discount is shown in the Order Summary and deducted from the total.
⚠️
Caution Discounts cannot be changed after the order is placed. Double-check before tapping Place Order.

2.4 Completing a Delivery
1
Tap the Order Card
From the Driver Dashboard, tap the order you are about to deliver to open the Delivery Detail screen.
2
Review Customer Info
The screen shows the customer's name, phone number (tap to call directly), and any delivery notes.
3
Check the Map Pin
A map shows the customer's delivery address. Use it to navigate.
4
Hand Over the Laundry
When you have given the laundry to the customer, tap the green Mark as Delivered button.
5
Tap "Back to Dashboard"
A confirmation screen appears. Tap Back to Dashboard to return to your delivery list.
📞
Calling the Customer — On the delivery detail screen, the customer's phone number is a tap-to-call link. Tap it to call them directly from your phone.

3.2 Managing Orders & Assigning Drivers

From the Admin Orders page, you can see all orders across every branch and assign drivers for delivery.

📋 Viewing Orders
  • Go to Orders in the sidebar
  • Orders from all branches are shown in one list
  • Filter by status: All, Pending, Confirmed, Processing, Ready, Completed, Cancelled
  • Click any order row to open the Order Detail Panel on the right
  • Order Detail shows: all items, customer info, branch, status history, and driver assignment
🚗 Assigning a Driver
1
Open the Order Detail
Click an order from the Orders list. The detail panel opens on the right side.
2
Find the "Assign Driver" Button
This button appears for orders with status: Confirmed, Processing, Ready, or Out for Delivery.
3
Select a Driver from the Dropdown
A dropdown shows drivers available at the branch. Select the driver.
4
Tap "Assign"
The order is now assigned. The driver will see it on their Driver App with a NEW badge.
